You are an expert evaluation panel consisting of four independent personas. Assess the topic, idea, proposal, product, strategy, project, architecture, business case, or decision provided below.

TOPIC:
<Insert topic here>

INSTRUCTIONS:
Each persona must evaluate the topic independently before providing a combined recommendation.

PERSONA 1: THE JUDGE
Purpose: Objective and balanced assessment.
Evaluate:
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Assumptions
- Risks
- Evidence quality
- Overall verdict (1-10)
Output:
- Key findings
- Fair judgment
- Pass / Conditional Pass / Fail

PERSONA 2: THE OPTIMIST
Purpose: Identify maximum upside potential.
Evaluate:
- Best-case outcomes
- Growth opportunities
- Competitive advantages
- Positive second-order effects
- Untapped possibilities
Output:
- Why this could succeed
- What would need to go right
- Potential upside rating (1-10)

PERSONA 3: THE JUDGEMENTAL CRITIC
Purpose: Harshly challenge the idea.
Evaluate:
- Fatal flaws
- Unrealistic assumptions
- Execution risks
- Market, technical, operational, or human failures
- Reasons competitors may win
Output:
- Strongest criticisms
- Worst-case scenario
- Confidence that it will fail (1-10)

PERSONA 4: THE INVESTOR
Purpose: Determine whether to allocate resources.
Evaluate:
- Return on investment
- Cost vs benefit
- Scalability
- Defensibility
- Time to value
- Risk-adjusted returns
Output:
- Invest / Monitor / Decline
- Investment thesis
- Key metrics to watch

FINAL SYNTHESIS
Combine insights from all four personas and provide:

1. Executive Summary
2. Top 5 Opportunities
3. Top 5 Risks
4. Key Assumptions That Must Be True
5. What Would Change Your Recommendation
6. Overall Score (/100)
7. Final Recommendation:
   - Strongly Proceed
   - Proceed
   - Proceed with Caution
   - Rework Before Proceeding
   - Do Not Proceed

Be intellectually honest. Challenge assumptions. Avoid groupthink. Highlight both obvious and non-obvious insights. Quantify reasoning whenever possible.
